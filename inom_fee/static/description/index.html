University — Fees

Fee structures, instalment schedules, scholarships and student fee accounts, built directly on Odoo accounting.

Odoo 19 · Community & Enterprise · Part of the INOM University Management Suite

Key features

Fee structures

Per programme, quota and academic year, with component heads of fee and an instalment plan that must total 100% before publishing.

Scholarships & waivers

Percentage or fixed-amount concessions applied to the student's account, capped at the gross fee.

Automatic fee accounts

Enrolling a student raises their fee account and dated instalment schedule from the published structure — without ever blocking the enrolment.

Real invoices

Each instalment is issued as a posted customer invoice; payments are standard Odoo payments, so the fee ledger always matches the trial balance.

Student & guardian portal

Students and guardians see their own instalments, balances and invoices, and download statements and receipts.

Printable reports

Fee structure sheet, student fee statement and fee receipt, rendered with the caller's own access rights.
