Approved Applied, Rejected Reverted

Approving applies the adjustment to stock while rejecting leaves it untouched - with full accountability on who decided each one.

When does an inventory adjustment need approval?

Only when it crosses the limits you set. Configure a quantity difference and/or value impact threshold, and adjustments at or above that limit are routed for approval - everything smaller applies instantly. You can also require approval for every adjustment if you prefer.
