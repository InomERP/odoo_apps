Key Highlights

Property Listing with Filters

A public Properties page lists published properties with picture, type, city, rooms, area and price, with search, rent or sale, category, type, bedrooms, city and price filters.

Property Detail Pages

Each property has its own page with a picture gallery, key facts, amenities and a public description. Owners, tenants, contracts and internal notes are never shown.

Enquiries Straight into CRM

The enquiry form creates a CRM enquiry linked to the property. A repeat enquiry from the same person is added to the open one instead of creating a duplicate.

Publishing Under Control

Only Property Managers publish properties, and a property disappears from the website as soon as it is rented, sold or archived.

Private Brochure Links

Generate a secret link to share a property page with a client before it is published.

Mobile Friendly

Listing, filters, detail pages and the enquiry form adapt to phones and tablets.

Interactive 3D View

Visitors rotate around the building, look at its floor plan from above and walk through the rooms. Upload your own 3D model, or let Odoo generate one from the property details.

3D View on the Property Page

Every property page has an interactive 3D view between the photos and the key facts. It loads only when the visitor scrolls to it.

3D Exterior: Apartment Building

An apartment tower with the property's number of floors, framed windows, balconies with glass railings and a rooftop, on its street.

3D Exterior: House or Villa

A house with a tiled roof, brick base, garage, garden path, hedges and a fenced plot sized from the land area.

3D Exterior: Office Building

A glass office building with a canopy at the entrance.

3D Exterior: Shop

A shop with a glass front, a striped awning and a sign.

3D Exterior: Warehouse

A warehouse with loading shutters and a pitched metal roof, sized from the built-up area.

3D Exterior: Plot of Land

A fenced plot with corner markers and a sign board. Plots show the exterior only.

3D Interior: Floor Plan

The Interior mode shows a cut-away floor plan from above, laid out from the bedrooms, bathrooms and area, with each room's name and size.

3D Interior: Floor Switcher

Houses with several storeys have a floor switcher: bedrooms upstairs, living spaces and the staircase downstairs.

3D Interior: Office Layout

Offices are laid out with an open workspace, a meeting room, a manager's cabin, a pantry and a restroom.

Walk Through: Living Room

Explore at eye height: drag to look around, walk with W A S D or the arrow keys, or tap the floor to go there. Walls stop you, doorways let you through.

Walk Through: Bedroom

Room buttons take the visitor straight into a room. Furniture follows the furnishing status: fully furnished, semi-furnished or unfurnished.

Walk Through: Kitchen

The kitchen with counter, hob, sink, fridge and a breakfast table.

Walk Through: Bathroom

Bathrooms with tiled walls and floor, WC, basin with mirror and a glass shower.

Walk Through: Office

Desks with screens and chairs in the open workspace of an office.

Walk Through: Shop

Stocked shelves on the sales floor of a shop, seen from the entrance.

Walk Through: Warehouse

Pallet racks in the storage hall of a warehouse.

3D View on Mobile

The 3D view works on phones and tablets: drag, pinch to zoom and tap rooms.

3D View Settings

On the property's Website tab: show or hide the 3D view and the interior, choose the building style and floors, or upload your own 3D model as a GLB file to show the real building.
