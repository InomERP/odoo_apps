Key Highlights

Full Repair Order Workflow

A six-stage pipeline — Repair Request, Under Diagnosis, Quotation Sent, Quotation Approved, In Work Order, Done — with search, filters, a list or Kanban board and CSV export, plus one-click Print Job Card and WhatsApp Customer, all as custom website pages.

Diagnosis, Work Orders & Labour Tracking

Diagnoses auto-detect Part vs. Labour straight from the product catalogue, work orders run Ready → In Progress → Finished with pause and resume, and every timesheet entry rolls up automatically into the repair order's logged hours and cost.

Customer Self-Service Portal

Customers request service, track live status and view QR-coded vehicle history from their own portal, and leave a star rating through a no-login feedback link the moment their order is marked Done.

Garage Management Portal

Repair orders, diagnosis, work orders, appointments, reminders and a customer portal — one workshop app built entirely as custom website pages, no backend screens required.

  • Screenshots

Live Operations Dashboard

One glance from Repair Orders, In Progress and Completed to Revenue, Reminders Due, Low-Stock and Avg. Rating, plus Orders by Status, Recent Repair Orders, Top Technicians, Top Parts Used, Slow-Moving Parts, Revenue Trend and the full Repair Funnel — every figure computed live from real records and filterable by All time, This month or This week.

Repair Orders — Search, Filter, Sort & Export

Every repair order lives on one website page with instant search by reference, customer, plate or complaint, tabs for All, Repair Request, In Work Order, Done and Cancelled, a sortable list or Kanban board, and one-click CSV export — no backend screens needed.

Repair Order — Six-Stage Workflow at a Glance

A single order page carries the job from Repair Request through Under Diagnosis, Quotation Sent and Quotation Approved to In Work Order and Done, with Mark Done / Cancel, Print Job Card and WhatsApp Customer actions, live counters for Diagnosis, Work Orders and Invoices, full customer and vehicle details, and tabs for Requested Services, Checklist, Vehicle Photos, Customer Feedback and Labour Log.

Diagnosis With Auto Type Detection

Technicians log the reported symptom and diagnosis result, then add spare parts and labour from the product catalogue — the type field automatically distinguishes Part from Labour, price and description pull straight from the product, and a labour log with technician, hours and rate feeds the estimated total.

Work Orders — Ready, In Progress, Finished

Each work order moves through Pause, Set Pending, Finish and Cancel, tracks Planned Start, Actual Start, Finished On and Duration automatically, and lets technicians log hours and rate straight into the labour timesheet, which rolls up into the repair order's logged hours and cost.

Before & After Vehicle Photos

Photos can be titled and uploaded straight onto the repair order — before, during or after service — building a visual record of the work that's attached to the job and available to the customer, without leaving the order page.

Printable Job Card

A one-click PDF Job Card lays out customer and vehicle details, the complaint, the full parts-and-services breakdown with the estimated total, and signature lines for both customer and technician — ready to print and hand over at intake or handover.

Automatic Review Request on Completion

The Customer Feedback tab shows the review status right on the order — a request is sent automatically the moment the order is marked Done, so feedback collection needs no manual follow-up.

Reviews — Every Rating Tied to Its Order

All customer reviews land in one searchable, exportable list with the repair order, customer, star rating, feedback text and date — each one traceable back to the exact job it was left for.

Appointments — One Calendar for All Bookings

A month calendar view lists every booking by customer and time, with a searchable list view alongside it, CSV export, and one click to create a new appointment.

Service Reminders by Date & Odometer

Reminders scheduled automatically when a job closes are tracked here by vehicle, customer, due date and due odometer reading, with a one-click Notify action to send the follow-up the moment it's due.

Low-Stock Alerts on Spare Parts

A daily check compares on-hand quantity against the minimum for every spare part; anything below threshold is flagged here with the shortfall and a Reordered action, so parts don't run out mid-job.

Configuration — Warranty Plans, Checklists, Time Slots & More

Warranty plans, service types, intake checklists, appointment time slots and spare parts are all configured from one Configuration hub with simple add-and-edit tables — no backend developer needed to adjust workshop settings.

Team & Role-Based Access

Four roles — Workshop Technician, Lead Technician, Service Advisor and Garage Administrator — are assigned per team member right from this page, with an Active / Archive status, so each member only sees and changes what their role allows.

My Garage — Customer Self-Service Portal

Customers signing into their own portal see a welcome dashboard with a Request a Service button, their repair history with live status, and their vehicles — full self-service without contacting the workshop for updates.

Customers Request Service Themselves

From the portal, a customer picks their vehicle (or types in one that isn't listed yet), describes the issue, and books a preferred date and time slot — the request lands directly in the workshop's queue.

Public Booking Page — No Login Needed

Anyone can request an appointment straight from the website with name, phone, email, car details, the issue, and a preferred date and time slot — no account or login required to get on the schedule.
