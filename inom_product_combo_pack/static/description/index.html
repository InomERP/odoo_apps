Product Combo & Bundle Pack Management

Product Combo Pack

The Product Combo Pack module lets you group multiple products into a single pack and add it to sales or purchase orders in one click. The pack carries the price while its component products are delivered and received automatically. With automatic or manual pack pricing, live stock availability, built-in margin analysis, and both combined and exploded modes, the module streamlines bundle selling, keeps inventory accurate, and ensures correct invoicing and vendor billing across the full order cycle.

Key Highlights

Auto Pricing & Margin Analysis

Bundle multiple products into one pack on the dedicated Combo Pack tab. Set the price manually or auto-calculate it from the components, and instantly see live availability and profit margin right on the product.

Automatic Delivery & Receipt

On order confirmation, only the real component products flow to the delivery or receipt — never the pack itself — so your warehouse always handles the correct physical items.

Combined & Exploded Modes

Add the pack as a single priced line with components attached, or explode it into separate priced lines for each item. Works on both sales and purchase orders with accurate ordered-quantity billing.

Streamlined Combo Pack Management

Bundle multiple products into a single pack and add it to sales or purchase orders in one click. Automate delivery and receipt of components, track live availability and margin, and ensure accurate invoicing from order creation to final billing with complete control.

Define a Combo Pack with Auto Pricing & Margin

Turn any product into a combo pack from the dedicated Combo Pack tab. Enable the pack, add your component products with quantities, and let the price auto-calculate from the items — while live stock availability and profit margin appear instantly on the same screen.

Real-Time Availability & Profit Insights

Know exactly how many complete packs your current stock can fulfil, backed by a clear availability badge. The module also displays total component cost, pack margin, and margin percentage, giving you instant profit visibility before you sell.

Quickly Filter All Combo Packs

A dedicated Combo Packs filter on the product views lets you list and manage every bundle product in a single click, keeping your catalog organized as your offerings grow.

Add a Full Pack in One Click

A dynamic wizard lets you choose the pack, set the quantity, and adjust the pack price and component prices on the fly — before adding everything to the order in one step, with no manual line entry.

Sales Order — Pack Line with Auto Components

In normal mode, the pack is added as a single priced line while its component products are attached automatically. The pack line carries the total price, and both Combo and Exploded options are always one click away.

Automatic Delivery of Pack Components

When the sales order is confirmed, only the real component products flow to the delivery — never the abstract pack. Your warehouse team always picks and ships the correct physical items.

Exploded Mode — Each Item as a Separate Line

Prefer itemized orders? Exploded mode adds every component as its own line with an individual price, instead of one combined pack line — ideal for detailed quotations and invoices.

Enable Combo Packs on Purchases

Activate combo packs for purchasing with a single toggle in the Purchase settings. Once enabled, the same combo pack buttons appear on purchase orders for effortless vendor ordering.

Automatic Receipt of Pack Components

Order kits from your vendors and receive the individual components automatically. On confirmation, only the component products appear on the incoming receipt — never the pack itself.
