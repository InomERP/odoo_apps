Vendor Product Management

Manage vendor products, supplier pricing, product mapping, and vendor-specific product codes from a centralized list.

Vendor Stock Management

Monitor vendor inventory across multiple vendor locations, track available quantities, detect unit of measure mismatches, and keep supplier stock information accurate with real-time updates.

Vendor Product Details

View vendor information, supplier pricing, matched Odoo products, and stock across multiple vendor locations. Track stock updates, monitor price changes through the activity log, and manage vendor inventory from a single product record.

Vendor Purchase Information

Manage multiple vendors for a product from the Purchase tab. Configure vendor-specific pricing, minimum purchase quantities, currencies, and delivery lead times to simplify purchasing and supplier management.

Import Vendor Products

Import vendor products in bulk using Excel files. Select a vendor, upload your spreadsheet, configure import options, archive obsolete products, mark previous prices as outdated, and update your vendor catalog in just a few clicks.

Import Vendor Stocks

Import vendor stock levels in bulk using Excel files. Select a vendor, upload your stock data, configure import options, archive obsolete stock records, and update inventory across multiple vendor locations with a single import.

Vendor Locations

Manage multiple locations for each vendor from a centralized view. Track supplier warehouses, monitor average delivery times, and maintain location-specific information to improve purchasing and inventory planning.

Vendor Product Management Settings

Configure vendor portal access, enable product and stock management, upload Excel import templates, and customize help instructions to simplify vendor onboarding and data imports.

Vendor Notification Preferences

Stay informed with vendor-related notifications. Subscribe to new vendor products, price updates, stock updates, discussions, activities, and notes to keep your team updated with the latest supplier changes.

Vendor Product Notifications

Subscribe to vendor product notifications to receive updates about new products, price changes, stock updates, discussions, activities, and notes. Stay informed whenever supplier information changes.

Vendor Stock Import Portal

Allow vendors to securely import their stock levels directly from the Odoo portal using a predefined spreadsheet template. The portal validates uploaded data, supports selective imports, and provides options to archive outdated stock records, making stock synchronization simple and efficient.
