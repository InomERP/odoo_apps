Key Highlights

Property Portfolio

Properties for rent or sale with type, address, rooms, area, amenities, pictures and documents. Availability is tracked from Draft to Available, Booked, Rented and Sold.

Owners, Tenants & Buyers

Owners, tenants and buyers are standard Odoo contacts, with their properties, contracts and invoices one click away, and their identity documents kept private.

Rental Contracts

Leases with approval, start, renewal, early termination and closing. Overlapping leases on the same property are refused, and every change is kept in the history.

Rent Invoicing & Deposits

A billing schedule per lease creates rent invoices in standard Odoo Invoicing. Track collected and overdue rent, and invoice, hold and settle security deposits.

Property Sales & CRM Enquiries

Rent and purchase enquiries in CRM with duplicate detection, then booking and sale contracts with booking and balance invoices.

Dashboard, Reports & Reminders

A dashboard with occupancy, income chart, calendar and follow-up lists, pivot reports on income, expiry and occupancy, and automatic reminder activities.

Configure Real Estate Settings

Choose whether leases need a manager's approval and tenants an identity document, set the reminder days, and pick the rent, deposit and sale products, the journal and automatic posting of rent invoices.

Property Types

Define the property types you manage, each in a category: residential, commercial, land or industrial.

Property Amenities

List the amenities your properties can offer, such as parking, lift, gym or swimming pool.

Property Portfolio

All properties with picture, type, city, rooms, area, price and availability, filtered by category and status.

Properties List View

The same portfolio as a list with owner, city, rental and sale prices and availability.

Create a Property: Details

Enter the name, type, whether it is for rent or sale, the owner, the address, rooms, area and furnishing.

Create a Property: Pricing

Set the monthly rent (or the sale price) and whether several leases may run on it at once.

Create a Property: Amenities

Tag the amenities the property offers.

Property Saved as Draft

The property gets its reference and starts in Draft until it is ready to let or sell.

Set Available

Set Available opens the property for leases, sales and enquiries. Each status change is recorded in the chatter.

Property Image Gallery

Add several pictures to the property; they are also used on the website.

Property Description

An internal description of the property, never published on the website.

Property Analysis

Count properties by type and availability in a pivot table or chart.

Property Owners

Owner Contact

Owner's Properties

Property Tenants

Tenants with their contact details and number of rental contracts.

Tenant Rental History

Every lease of the tenant, with property, dates, rent and status.

Tenant Identity Document

Upload an identity document with its number and expiry date. When enabled in the settings, a lease cannot start without a valid one.

Owner & Tenant Documents

All documents in one place, visible only to Real Estate users.

Property Buyers

Buyers of properties for sale.

Record an Enquiry

From the property, New Enquiry records the customer, contact details and asking price.

Enquiry Created

The enquiry is a CRM opportunity linked to the property, with a follow-up activity planned for the salesperson.

Duplicate Enquiry Warning

If the customer already has an open enquiry for the property, Odoo warns you: open the existing one or create a new one anyway.

Rental Enquiries Pipeline

Rental enquiries move through the CRM stages.

Sale Enquiries Pipeline

Enquiry Analysis

Enquiries per property and status.

Step 1: Convert the Enquiry

Create Rental Contract turns the rental enquiry into a draft lease.

Step 2: Draft Rental Contract

Property, tenant, dates and rent come from the enquiry and the property; enter the security deposit.

Step 3: Approve

A Property Manager approves the lease, which reserves the property.

Step 4: Start the Contract

The lease is running, the property becomes Rented and the billing schedule lists every rent period.

Step 5: Invoice Due Rent

Invoice Due Rent creates the invoice of each period that is due (a daily job does it automatically too).

Step 6: Rent Invoice

A standard customer invoice for the rent period, left in draft for review unless automatic posting is enabled.

Step 7: Confirm the Invoice

The invoice is posted in accounting.

Step 8: Register the Payment

Record the tenant's payment with the standard payment wizard.

Step 9: Rent Paid

The invoice is paid and the billing period is marked paid.

Step 10: Invoice the Deposit

Invoice Deposit creates the security deposit invoice for the tenant.

Step 11: Lease Figures

Rent invoiced, collected and overdue, and the deposit invoiced, received and held, all from accounting.

Step 12: Lease Payments

All payments received for the lease.

Step 13: Property Rented

The property shows as Rented while the lease runs.

Rent Due

Rent periods due soon or overdue that are not yet paid.

Billing Schedule

Every rent period of every lease, with an Invoice button to invoice a period by hand.

Rental Invoices

All rent and deposit invoices.

Overdue Rent

Posted rent invoices past their due date.

Paid Rent Invoices

Rent invoices already paid.

Outstanding Rent

Rent invoices still to be paid.

Deposit Status

For each lease: deposit invoiced, received, held, deducted, refunded and its status.

Lease Ending Soon

A reminder activity is created before the lease ends, so it can be renewed, closed or terminated in time.

Renew the Lease

Choose the new dates and a rent increase; the new monthly rent is calculated.

Renewal Contract

The renewal is a new draft contract linked to the previous one, which keeps its history.

Terminate Early

Record the termination date, the reason and details.

Confirm Termination

Odoo asks for confirmation, as the property becomes available again.

Lease Terminated

Periods invoiced after the end date are flagged in the chatter so they can be credited.

Settle the Deposit

Enter deductions such as repairs; Odoo shows what is left to refund.

Settlement Credit Note

A credit note releases the deposit held, less the deductions.

Confirm the Credit Note

The settlement is posted in accounting.

Refund the Tenant

Pay the refund with the standard payment wizard.

Deposit Settled

The lease shows the deposit deducted and refunded, and its status Settled.

Close a Lease

Close Contract ends a running or expired lease and frees the property.

Closed Lease History

Approval, renewal and end-of-contract details are kept on the History tab.

Rental Contracts

All leases with dates, days left, rent and status.

Rental Analysis

Monthly rent by property and status.

Sale Step 1: Sale Enquiry

Create Property Sale turns a purchase enquiry into a sale contract.

Sale Step 2: Draft Sale Contract

The agreed price comes from the property; enter the booking amount.

Sale Step 3: Confirm Booking

Booking reserves the property for the buyer.

Sale Step 4: Booked

The contract is booked, ready to invoice the booking amount.

Sale Step 5: Property Booked

The property can no longer be booked or let to someone else.

Sale Step 6: Booking Invoice

Invoice Booking Amount creates the customer invoice for the booking.

Sale Step 7: Confirm

The booking invoice is posted.

Sale Step 8: Booking Paid

The buyer's booking payment is registered.

Sale Step 9: Balance Invoice

Invoice Balance invoices the rest of the price, with the agreed payment terms.

Sale Step 10: Fully Invoiced

Invoices and payments are listed on the contract.

Sale Step 11: Mark as Sold

Once fully invoiced, the contract is marked sold.

Sale Step 12: Property Sold

The property is now Sold.

Sale Summary Report

Print a sale summary with prices, invoices and payments.

Sale Contracts

All sale contracts with invoicing and status.

Sale Analysis

Agreed price, amount invoiced and paid by property and status.

Rental Income by Property

Rent invoiced, paid and outstanding per property, straight from accounting.

Rental Income Chart

The same figures as a chart.

Rental Income by Owner

Rental income grouped by owner.

Tenant Outstanding Invoices

Unpaid rent per tenant with days overdue.

Contract Expiry

Running leases grouped by the month they end.

Property Occupancy

Properties by type and availability.

Sale Transactions

Every property sale with buyer, owner, amounts and status.

Real Estate Dashboard

Availability cards, rental income chart, property status, leases and sales, calendar, recent activity, quick actions and follow-up lists.

Dashboard Period Filter

Switch the period, for example This Year; income and sales follow the period.

Dashboard Income Chart Range

Show rent collected against invoiced over 30 days, 6 months or 12 months.

Dashboard Quick Actions

Quick actions open a new property, a new lease, outstanding rent or the income report in one click.

Automatic Reminders

A daily job creates To-Do activities for rent due, overdue rent, leases ending and pending enquiries, once each.

Rent invoices are created by a daily scheduled job from each lease's billing schedule. Choose in Real Estate > Configuration > Settings whether they are posted automatically or left in draft for review.
