Complete Asset Registry with Built-in Analytics

Track every asset from a single powerful record. Link work orders, requests, transfers, inspections and meters through smart buttons, monitor health score, MTBF, MTTR and total maintenance cost in real time, and stay on top of warranty status automatically.

Visual Asset Board with Live Health Scores

Get an instant overview of your entire asset fleet with a kanban board grouped by status. Every card shows the asset reference, category, location and a live health score, so you can spot assets needing attention at a glance.

Printable QR-Coded Asset Labels

Generate professional QR-coded labels for every asset with a single click. Scan the code to instantly pull up the asset record — perfect for field technicians, audits and physical inventory checks.

Safety-First: Work Cannot Start Without a Permit

Enforce workplace safety with a hard permit gate. Any work order flagged as permit-required is physically blocked from starting until an approved safety permit exists — no exceptions, no unsafe shortcuts.

Permit to Work with LOTO & Multi-Level Approval

Manage six permit types — Lockout/Tagout, Hot Work, Electrical, Work at Height, Confined Space and General Work — through a full Draft to Approved to Active workflow, complete with hazard tracking, safety precautions and LOTO isolation points.

Full Work Order Lifecycle with Mandatory Checklists

Drive maintenance from request to closure. Track labor, spare parts and failure analysis, enforce mandatory checklist completion before work can finish, consume parts through real stock moves, and keep every safety permit linked with a full audit trail.

Reliability Analytics on Every Asset

Make data-driven maintenance decisions with automatically calculated MTBF, MTTR, health score and lifetime maintenance cost. Watch asset status update in real time as work orders progress from active to under maintenance.

Inspections That Create Their Own Follow-ups

Build inspection templates with pass/fail, numeric (min/max) and observation checkpoints. Numeric readings are auto-graded, and any failed inspection automatically generates a follow-up maintenance request — nothing ever slips through.

Asset Meters with Secure IoT Ingestion

Record cumulative and gauge meter readings manually or push them automatically through a token-secured IoT REST endpoint. Readings feed meter-based preventive plans and threshold alerts, keeping maintenance perfectly aligned with actual usage.

Built-in Depreciation Engine — No Enterprise Needed

Calculate straight-line and declining-balance depreciation over a full multi-year schedule, with automatic monthly posting via cron, salvage value handling and live book value on every asset. A powerful accounting feature delivered entirely on Odoo Community.

What does this module do and does it need Odoo Enterprise?

AssetCare is a complete asset and maintenance suite. It gives you a QR-coded asset registry with parent/child hierarchy, custody transfers with approval, a full request-to-work-order lifecycle with checklists and real spare-part consumption, a Permit-to-Work safety layer (including LOTO) that blocks unsafe work, inspection templates that auto-create follow-up requests, cumulative and gauge meters with a token-secured IoT REST endpoint, time-based and meter-based preventive plans, and a built-in straight-line and declining-balance depreciation engine with automatic monthly posting. It also includes MTBF, MTTR and health-score analytics plus WhatsApp deep-link alerts. Built for Odoo 19, Odoo 18 and Odoo 17, it uses only the standard Base, Mail, HR, Stock and Portal apps — no Odoo Enterprise and no third-party dependency required.
