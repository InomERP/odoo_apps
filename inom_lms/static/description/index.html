University — Learning

Online courses, lessons, assignments and graded submissions for your students, delivered through the university portal.

Odoo 19 · Community & Enterprise · Part of the INOM University Management Suite

Key features

Courses

Per department, linked to a subject and the teaching faculty member. Draft courses stay invisible to students, enforced by a record rule.

Rich lessons

Readings, documents, videos and links. YouTube and Vimeo are embedded, video files play inline and PDFs open in the page.

Secure lesson files

Uploaded files are served only after the student's access to that lesson is checked — never through a generic download link.

Assignments

Issue and due dates, maximum marks and live counts of submitted and graded work.

Grading & feedback

Record a mark with a line of feedback in one step, or return work for rework. Late submissions are flagged automatically.

Grader-only marks

Only faculty, HoD, dean or registrar can change a mark or status — enforced on the model, not just hidden in the screen.
